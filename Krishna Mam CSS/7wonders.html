The 7 Wonders

The Great Wall of China is very long and big. It is made of heavy stone and brick. People walk on it today to see the land.

Petra is a very old city hidden in the desert. It is cut into big red rocks. It is located in a country called Jordan.

The Colosseum is a giant round building. It is standing in Rome, Italy. Many people watched big games there a long time ago.

Chichen Itza is a large step pyramid. It has many stone steps going up to the top. It was built in Mexico by the Maya people.

Machu Picchu is a lost city high up on a green mountain. It is often hidden in the clouds. You can find it in Peru.

The Taj Mahal is a beautiful building in India. It is made of bright white stone. A king built it because he loved his wife very much.

Christ the Redeemer is a very tall statue. It has wide open arms and looks over the city. It is located in Brazil.

All of these amazing places are very beautiful and special. Many people travel from far away to see them every single year.
